How these codes work

Do vendor sales stack with SAMMYC?

Sometimes. Where a vendor has confirmed it, we list the sale and the code as two separate lines so you can see exactly what each one takes off. We never publish a single combined percentage, because the order the discounts apply in changes the final number and only the vendor checkout can settle it.

Why do some offers have no end date?

Those are standing discounts rather than a limited sale. They are open ended as far as we know, but a vendor can withdraw one at any time, so treat the checkout total as the answer.

How often is this page updated?

Every time a vendor adds, changes or ends an offer, which in practice is most days. The date above is when the list was last checked, and expired offers drop off on their own.

Does a bigger discount mean a better price?

No, and this is the most common mistake. A 40 percent discount off a high list price often lands above a 15 percent discount somewhere cheaper. Use the cost per mg comparison to see where the money actually goes.

What does MyPeptidePrice earn from these?

We earn a commission when you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. It does not change which offers appear here or how they are ordered. Offers are listed by how much they take off, not by what they pay us.

Are these products for human use?

No. Everything listed on this site is sold for laboratory research purposes only and is not for human or animal consumption. See our research disclaimer.
